Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find the right camera for my needs?

Start by chatting with Ahri, our AI assistant, on the home page. Tell Ahri your budget, experience level, and what you want to shoot. You can also browse our camera database with filters or read our buying guides.

Are the camera recommendations sponsored?

No. Our recommendations are based on specifications, expert reviews, user ratings, and real-world performance. While we may include affiliate links for purchases, they do not influence our recommendations.

Can I request a camera to be added to the database?

Absolutely! Email us at support@lenslink.ai with the camera model you'd like to see added. We regularly update our database with new releases and popular requests.

Do you offer camera rentals or sales?

We're a comparison and recommendation platform, not a retailer. However, we provide links to trusted retailers where you can purchase cameras at competitive prices.

How can I report incorrect information?

We strive for accuracy! If you spot an error in our camera specs or descriptions, please email us at support@lenslink.ai with details, and we'll correct it promptly.

Can I contribute reviews or articles?

We welcome contributions from photographers and camera enthusiasts! Contact us at partnerships@lenslink.ai with your proposal.
